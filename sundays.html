Sundays

What happens on a Sunday

Two services in two languages. This page is here because people ask — not because coming is the point of anything else on this site.

Times

What it is like

Songs, a reading, about sixty minutes of teaching, and coffee afterwards. The eight PM o’clock is in English / Bahasa; they are with translations of each other, they are two congregations.

Nobody will ask you to stand up, give money or fill anything in. Children stay with you or go to their groups, whichever you prefer.
