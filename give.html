Give

Money, goods, or two hours

All three are genuinely useful, and we would rather tell you which one is short this week than pretend they are equal.

Right now

What the shelves are short of

Drop-off is at the church entrance, Tuesday to Thursday between nine and four. Please do not leave anything outside overnight — it gets taken or ruined, and then somebody has to clear it up.

Money

Where a dollar goes

We buy through reliable networks, which turns financial support into meaningful provisions. Giving money generally offers flexible purchasing power — but bring items directly if that is easier for you.

By cheque or in person

Contributions can be handed in at the office or processed via our direct support channels with minimal overhead.

Monthly

A standing order is what lets the pantry order stock in advance instead of buying week to week. Ask the office for the form.

If you use the pantry

Please do not give money. Several people have tried and we have sent it back. Come and eat on a Wednesday instead.
