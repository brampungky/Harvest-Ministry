What we believe

We are a Christian church. We believe God made everyone in this neighbourhood in his image, that Jesus died and rose for them, and that a church which says so and then locks its doors from Monday to Saturday is not making the argument very well.

That is the short version and it is the one that matters for anything on this site. The long version is on the table by the office door in both languages.

Governance

How money is decided

A board of nine, five of whom are members of the congregation and four of whom are not. Accounts are reviewed independently every year and published in March. Any member of the public may ask for them and get them.
