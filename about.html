Our Story

It Began with a Deep Longing

Harvest Ministry began with a deep longing in the heart of a young man named Sutrisno Njakatara, a migrant from Sumbawa who came to Nusa Lembongan.

At that time, many people from Sumbawa who had moved to Nusa Lembongan longed for a place where they could come together in praise, worship, and fellowship. In the midst of the negative stigma often associated with newcomers, Sutrisno saw something different. He saw children of God who were hungry and thirsty for His Word—souls who longed to know Christ and grow in Him.

That longing eventually became a calling. With a steadfast heart, Sutrisno decided to continue his education, holding on to one conviction: one day, he would return to Nusa Lembongan to pastor the congregation there.

Returning to Serve

The journey toward fulfilling that calling was not easy. Through a long and difficult season filled with sacrifice and perseverance, he eventually completed his education. And just as he had promised, he returned to Nusa Lembongan.

Without a large team, without a church building, and with nothing but complete dependence on God, he began the ministry through a small prayer fellowship. From that small fellowship, God gradually began to grow a congregation. Eventually, a small church was formed, and it became known as Harvest Ministry.

It Is Not About Being Big or Small

In a world that has become increasingly competitive, where many churches have grown with magnificent buildings, extensive facilities, and large-scale ministries, there are still places that may easily be overlooked. Nusa Lembongan is one of them.

But for Pastor Sutrisno, ministry has never been about finding the biggest place or the largest congregation. He does not see a place as “fertile ground” or “dry ground.” He simply desires to obey God's voice and serve the people whom God has entrusted to him.

And so, he continues to stand behind the pulpit. Even today, Harvest Ministry worships in the open air, without walls and without a church building. Under the open sky, we continue to lift up our praise and proclaim the Word of God.

And the ministry is no longer limited to people from Sumbawa. Today, Harvest Ministry welcomes every soul who longs to seek God—regardless of where they come from, their ethnicity, nationality, or background. Whether they are from Java, Sumbawa, Bali, or from different parts of the world, everyone is welcome to worship, fellowship, and grow together. Even travelers and visitors from other countries are warmly welcomed.

Because for us, church is not first and foremost about a building. Church is about people who are seeking God. And in this simple place, we believe that God is building something far greater than a building.

A family.
A community.
A place for every soul to encounter Christ.

This is the story of Harvest Ministry. Not because we have everything. But because we believe that when God calls, He will also provide His presence and lead the way.

Berawal dari Sebuah Kerinduan

Harvest Ministry berawal dari sebuah kerinduan yang mendalam dalam hati seorang pemuda bernama Sutrisno Njakatara, seorang perantau dari Sumbawa yang datang ke Nusa Lembongan.

Pada waktu itu, banyak perantau dari Sumbawa yang memiliki kerinduan untuk berkumpul bersama dalam pujian, penyembahan, dan persekutuan. Di tengah berbagai stigma negatif yang sering melekat kepada para pendatang, Sutrisno justru melihat sesuatu yang berbeda. Ia melihat anak-anak Tuhan yang haus dan lapar akan Firman Tuhan—jiwa-jiwa yang rindu mengenal dan bertumbuh di dalam Kristus.

Kerinduan itu kemudian menjadi sebuah panggilan. Dengan keteguhan hati, Sutrisno memutuskan untuk melanjutkan pendidikannya, dengan satu tekad: suatu hari ia akan kembali ke Nusa Lembongan untuk menggembalakan jemaat di sana.

Kembali untuk Melayani

Perjalanan untuk mewujudkan tekad tersebut tidaklah mudah. Dengan perjuangan yang panjang dan penuh pengorbanan, ia akhirnya berhasil menyelesaikan pendidikannya. Dan sebagaimana yang telah ia tekadkan, ia kembali ke Nusa Lembongan.

Tanpa sebuah tim besar, tanpa fasilitas yang memadai, dan hanya dengan berserah sepenuhnya kepada Tuhan, ia mengawali pelayanan melalui sebuah persekutuan doa. Dari sebuah persekutuan kecil itu, Tuhan perlahan menumbuhkan sebuah jemaat. Hingga akhirnya lahirlah sebuah gereja kecil yang kemudian dikenal sebagai Harvest Ministry.

Bukan Tentang Besar atau Kecil

Di tengah zaman yang semakin kompetitif, ketika banyak gereja bertumbuh dengan gedung yang megah, berbagai fasilitas, dan pelayanan yang besar, masih ada tempat-tempat yang mungkin luput dari perhatian. Nusa Lembongan adalah salah satunya.

Namun bagi Pdt. Sutrisno, pelayanan bukan tentang mencari tempat yang besar atau jemaat yang banyak. Ia tidak melihat sebuah tempat sebagai “lahan basah” atau “lahan kering”. Ia hanya ingin menaati suara Tuhan dan melayani jiwa-jiwa yang dipercayakan kepadanya.

Karena itu, ia tetap berdiri di mimbar. Bahkan hingga hari ini, Harvest Ministry masih beribadah di ruang terbuka, tanpa dinding dan tanpa gedung gereja. Di bawah langit terbuka, pujian tetap dinaikkan dan Firman Tuhan tetap diberitakan.

Dan pelayanan ini tidak lagi terbatas bagi para perantau dari Sumbawa. Hari ini, Harvest Ministry membuka pintunya bagi setiap jiwa yang rindu mencari Tuhan—tanpa memandang dari mana mereka berasal, suku, bangsa, maupun latar belakang mereka. Baik dari Jawa, Sumbawa, Bali, maupun dari berbagai belahan dunia. Bahkan siapa pun yang datang sebagai pendatang atau wisatawan, termasuk para foreigners, dipersilakan untuk bersekutu dan bertumbuh bersama.

Sebab bagi kami, gereja bukan pertama-tama tentang bangunan. Gereja adalah tentang orang-orang yang mencari Tuhan. Dan di tempat yang sederhana ini, kami percaya Tuhan sedang membangun sesuatu yang jauh lebih besar daripada sebuah gedung.

Sebuah keluarga.
Sebuah komunitas.
Sebuah tempat bagi setiap jiwa untuk mengenal Kristus.

Inilah perjalanan Harvest Ministry. Bukan karena kami memiliki segala sesuatu. Tetapi karena kami percaya bahwa ketika Tuhan memanggil, Ia juga akan menyertai.

In short

What we believe

We are a Christian church. We believe God made everyone in this neighbourhood in his image, that Jesus died and rose for them, and that a church which says so and then locks its doors from Monday to Saturday is not making the argument very well.

That is the short version and it is the one that matters for anything on this site. The long version is on the table by the office door in both languages.

Governance

How money is decided

A board of nine, five of whom are members of the congregation and four of whom are not. Accounts are reviewed independently every year and published in March. Any member of the public may ask for them and get them.
