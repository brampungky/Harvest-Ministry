What we will and will not ask you

No paperwork

No ID, no proof of address, no proof of income for the pantry, the clothing room or the meal. Bring the bill for help with bills — that is all.

No questions about status

We do not ask about immigration status, we do not record it, and we have never given information about anyone who came here to anyone who asked for it.

Nothing is expected of you

Nothing here requires you to attend anything, believe anything or sit through anything. If you want to talk about faith, ask — and if you do not, nobody will raise it.
