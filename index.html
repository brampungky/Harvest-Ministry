When the doors are open

Closed on federal holidays. If the schools are closed for snow, so are we — check the phone message.

Before you come

Three things we want you to know

No paperwork

No ID, no proof of address, no proof of income for the pantry, the clothing room or the meal. Bring the bill for help with bills — that is all.

No questions about status

We do not ask about immigration status, we do not record it, and we have never given information about anyone who came here to anyone who asked for it.

Nothing is expected of you

Nothing here requires you to attend anything, believe anything or sit through anything. If you want to talk about faith, ask — and if you do not, nobody will raise it.

Last year

Last year in this building

Sundays

What a Sunday looks like

About seventy-five minutes: singing, a reading, a talk, prayers, and coffee afterwards for as long as anybody wants it. The nine o’clock is in English and the eleven is in Spanish; they are the same service, not a main one and a translation.

Nobody who uses anything else in this building is expected to come, and nobody who comes is ever asked what they used.
